# Executive Proposal & Risk Mitigation Framework: Strategic Target Adjustment

An enterprise-grade framework and operational report designed to justify, defend, and execute strategic target recalibration and objective withdrawal.

## 📋 Overview

Pursuing unviable operational targets can severely strain organizational resources, induce severe equipment failures, increase defect rates, and introduce critical safety and SLA non-compliance risks. 

This repository contains the structured framework, trade-off models, feasibility proof criteria, and sign-off templates required to present a air-tight case to Executive Leadership (Board of Directors & C-Suite) for pivoting operational goals.

---

## 📄 Included File

* **`Bao_Cao_Giai_Trinh_Rut_Khoi_Muc_Tieu.md`**: The complete Vietnamese Markdown report fully populated with financial trade-off data, technical capacity metrics, supply chain bottleneck analyses, and inter-departmental sign-off matrix.

---

## 🛠 Framework Components

### 1. Trade-off Analysis
Evaluates the hidden costs of maintaining unrealistic targets versus the ROI of reallocating released capital and human resources.
* **Hidden Costs:** Overtime (OT) budget drain, machine breakdown probabilities, defect rate increases.
* **Resource Optimization:** Reallocation of skilled workforce and operational budget to high-ROI projects.

### 2. Feasibility Proof
Provides quantifiable physical and systemic limits to prove target infeasibility.
* **Technical Constraints:** Machine design capacity thresholds and physical thermal/maintenance limits.
* **Supply Chain Bottlenecks:** Extended lead times and component price fluctuations.
* **Historical Data:** Empirical evidence (e.g., Overall Equipment Effectiveness - OEE logs) proving systemic boundaries.

### 3. Risk Mitigation
Addresses critical compliance and risk metrics.
* **Occupational Safety:** OHSAS/ISO 45001 compliance and incident risk reduction.
* **Quality Assurance & SLA:** Brand protection, return rate management, and contractual SLA adherence.

### 4. Cross-Departmental Sign-Off Matrix
Structured decision matrix for key business stakeholders:
* Manufacturing / Operations Manager
* Quality Assurance (QA/QC) Manager
* Financial Controller / Chief Accountant
* Managing Director / Board of Directors

## 🚀 Usage Guide

1. Clone or copy `Bao_Cao_Giai_Trinh_Rut_Khoi_Muc_Tieu.md`.
2. Customize the bracketed parameters (`[X%]`, `[Nguồn lực]`, `[Tên người phê duyệt]`) to align with your organization's specific operational context.
3. Export to PDF using your preferred Markdown converter or WeasyPrint pipeline for formal presentation to Executive Leadership.
